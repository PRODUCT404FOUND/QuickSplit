🔐 Technical Innovation

Zero-Knowledge Security & Real-Time Sync Architecture

🔒

Zero-Knowledge Database Cryptography

Client-side E2EE ensures your financial and personal data never leaves your device unencrypted:

  • Client Key Derivation: Generated 12-char cryptographic key (e.g. QS-7K9A-2X4M).
  • Dynamic Salting & CRC: 8-char random salt + 16-bit CRC checksum prevents replay & rainbow attacks.
  • Zero Cloud Visibility: Firestore stores only { encData: "ENC$..." }. No plain-text names, amounts, or VPAs exist in the database.
  • Fragment-Only Transmission: Secret keys travel strictly in URL fragments (#group=...&key=...), never touching server logs.
⚡

Greedy Debt Simplification Algorithm

Optimizes complex group spending into minimum direct cash transfers:

  • Net Balance Vector: Compiles total paid vs total fair share for each member: Net[i] = Paid[i] - Share[i].
  • Two-Pointer Matching: Continuously pairs maximum debtor with maximum creditor until all balances clear.
  • Complexity: Compresses potential N*(N-1)/2 transactions down to at most N-1.
  • Dual-Mode Sync: Firestore real-time listener + offline localStorage fallback.
